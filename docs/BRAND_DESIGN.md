# StatCam Hockey Brand & UI Direction

## Product promise

**Record the shift. Capture the moment. Understand the game.**

StatCam Hockey combines live individual statistics with shift-linked video. The interface should feel like a focused sports camera and a clean performance dashboard, never like a dense scorekeeping form.

## Visual language

- Deep navy creates a low-glare rink and camera-monitor environment.
- Ice white and silver carry primary information.
- Electric blue identifies navigation, selection, film, and analysis.
- Green is reserved for on-ice, success, and positive actions.
- Red is reserved for stopping, failure, penalty, and destructive actions.
- Monospaced or tabular numerals are preferred for clocks and timing.
- Focus-frame, lens, and telemetry motifs may be used sparingly.

## Logo usage

- Use the full logo for the app icon, splash screen, loading screen, and onboarding.
- Use a small icon plus the `STATCAM HOCKEY` wordmark on Home.
- Do not place a large logo inside Live Game or Camera Shift Mode.
- Keep adequate clear space around the full logo and avoid placing it on bright imagery.

## Screen priorities

### Home and dashboards

Brand identity may be more visible. Cards should resemble clean performance panels with restrained blue highlights.

### Live Game

Clock, period, shift state, and the shift control remain visually dominant. Branding must not compete with one-tap recording.

### Camera Shift Mode

The camera image is the background. Controls use translucent dark surfaces, bright state colors, and large touch targets. Analytics stay out of the recording view.

### Review and film

Video, event bookmarks, and shift context form the primary hierarchy. Electric blue connects timeline events with video navigation.

## Interaction principles

- Preserve large glove-friendly touch targets.
- Use haptics as confirmation whenever possible.
- Keep all live actions within one tap.
- Keep all user-facing interface copy in English.
- Never require connectivity for live game tracking or saved shift review.
